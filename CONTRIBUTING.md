# Contributing

Owners/collaborators may use private planning issues with text and synthetic
reproduction details only. Do not
attach private media, credentials, device identifiers, or
machine-specific logs.

Follow the current authorization in `docs/PROJECT_PLAN.md`, add or update
meaningful tests, record verification in `docs/BUILD_NOTES.md`, and avoid
changing model thresholds or toolchain versions without a separately versioned
evaluation decision. Do not replace the owner-authorized bundled asset without
new authorization and identity. External redistribution still needs owner/legal,
independent-validation and physical-device review plus explicit release approval.
