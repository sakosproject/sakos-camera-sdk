Decide before an SDK-managed output is delivered

The private local SDK includes a bundled fixed-view runtime, managed in-memory photos, private staged video review and a functional Android sample. Approval stays tied to the capture under review.

Photo

Injected in-memory review passes a capture-bound Allow result to an approved-output sink. Block, Review and Failure are not delivered through that sink.

Video

Temporal planning samples a bounded timeline and can request confirmation around suspicious evidence. An Android decoder reviews planned timestamps from private no-backup staging. Non-approved and abandoned sessions request cleanup; cleanup failures block new recording.

Current boundary

Current checks use generated benign patterns, mocks, simulated scores and an isolated emulator scene. They establish mechanics, not accuracy, parity or physical-device behavior. Legal redistribution and external delivery remain gated. This static page is a local preview; the Android sample is separate.
