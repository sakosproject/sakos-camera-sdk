A local candidate for managed camera review

SakOS Camera is an independent Android project for managed photo and video review. Its current source candidate includes Fixed14 and Adaptive14 model runtimes, camera capture bridges, a sample app and local consumer packaging.

One device passed a live camera mechanics check

On October 2, 2026, an opt-in flow passed on a Samsung Galaxy S20 FE (SM-G781W, Android 13/API 33). It checked fresh-install permission gating, rear and front camera calibration, photo and video capture, selected controls, lifecycle recovery, cancellation, and private save/delete mechanics. The operator-approved views were benign office floors and ceilings. Test captures stayed on the phone and were deleted after review.

What the result supports

On that handset, the measured camera profiles and managed capture flow worked through the exercised paths. Unsupported or duplicate profiles stayed hidden.

What it does not support

This was one bounded hardware mechanics check. It does not establish classifier accuracy, broad device/API coverage, source parity, physical rotation behavior, or independent validation.

Release readiness

The SDK and bundled model are not released

For the selected replacement model, the repository license review records the published redistribution conditions as satisfied and the required notices are retained with the model artifacts. The historical import is documented separately by hash; its bytes are excluded and its exact source weights and conversion recipe remain unresolved. This is a repository assessment, not an independent legal opinion. An experimental SDK release still needs an approved version and delivery target, verified versioned artifacts, and accurate limitations. Broader device/API testing and independent efficacy assessment remain disclosed follow-up work.

The source repository is currently private, and Maven artifacts are local. The replacement model provenance and license evidence are documented in the repository and packaged with the model library. The source repository is currently private. This public status site is published separately, offers no SDK, model, or binary downloads, and makes no detection-accuracy or safety certification claim.

Current verification record

The selected replacement passed the clean non-device gate: 185 JVM tests, zero lint errors, local Maven packaging, a minified consumer, and inspection of 24 artifacts. No emulator or device tests were run for this model. Earlier synthetic Android tests used the previous model and do not validate the replacement.

Automated test inputs were generated benign patterns, mocks, and simulated scores. These checks establish implementation mechanics only.
