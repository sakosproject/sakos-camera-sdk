Safer capture starts before media leaves the device

SakOS Camera is an independent Android SDK project for managed photo and video review. Current work includes a bundled Fixed14/default and Adaptive14/optional runtime, managed photo/video capture, a private approved-media sample viewer and local consumer packaging.

Spatial photo and sampled video review

Fixed14 short-circuits on policy Block; optional Adaptive14 executes the source staged policy with a distinct approval identity. Video review samples a bounded timeline. First-run calibration verifies usable camera graphs; the private reviewed library requires capture-bound approval and explicit authorization for external saving. Current tests use benign generated patterns, mocks, simulated scores and an isolated emulator scene; no accuracy, parity or physical-device result is claimed.

In-memory photo review

Managed photo review uses a capture-bound Allow approval before an SDK-controlled output sink can receive a frame.

Temporal video review

Video planning samples a bounded timeline and follows suspicious evidence with confirmation samples.
